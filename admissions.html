Application Requirements

Please prepare the following materials for your application:

Completed application form

Official transcripts from current/previous schools

Standardized test scores (optional)

Two teacher recommendations

Personal statement or essay

Parent statement

Application fee ($75, waived for financial aid applicants)

Financial Aid

We believe that financial circumstances should never be a barrier to an exceptional education.

Need-Based Aid

We offer generous financial aid packages based on demonstrated need. Over 40% of our families receive some form of financial assistance.

  • Average award: $18,000/year
  • Covers tuition and fees
  • Renewable annually

Merit Scholarships

Outstanding students may be awarded merit scholarships based on academic achievement, leadership, and special talents.

  • Presidential Scholarship: Full tuition
  • Dean's Scholarship: $15,000/year
  • Arts & STEM Awards available
